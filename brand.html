Glimmerfold

Living foil cards. Turn any picture into a foil-embossed greeting card that opens in your hands, glimmers as it turns, and quietly comes to life — then send it with a link.

The name. Glimmer is the foil catching the light and the little movements that make every card feel alive. Fold is the card itself — paper that opens to a letter inside.
The mark. A card caught mid-open: a gold-foil cover swinging toward you, the cream page behind it, and the glimmer waiting inside. The fold in the wordmark is set in foil and shimmers every few seconds, the way the cards do.
